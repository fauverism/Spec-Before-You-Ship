Common questions
Is this tutorial really free?
Yes, all 11 lessons are free and always will be. No account required, no paywall.
Which AI tools does this work with?
BridgeSpec works alongside any AI coding tool—Claude, Copilot, Cursor, Windsurf, or anything else that reads files in your project.
Do I need prior experience with specs?
No. Lesson 01 starts from scratch and builds the habit from the ground up.
What's the companion skill?
It's a Claude Code skill that lives in your project and walks you through lessons interactively, adapting to your stack. Find it in each lesson's sidebar.
Can I use this with a team?
Absolutely. Specs and design docs are even more valuable in teams—and most of the patterns in this tutorial are designed to scale to collaborative workflows.
How long does the full tutorial take?
Each lesson is designed to be completable in 20–40 minutes. The full 11-lesson course can be done in a weekend, or stretched across a couple of weeks.
